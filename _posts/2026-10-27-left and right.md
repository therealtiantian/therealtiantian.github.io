If you think about the origin of this mess, it’s simply the seat allocation after the french revolution, when those who would like a completely new order sit on the left and those who want to keep a selection of the old structure on the right. Now obviously people fight over left or right with each one’s own imagination of their meanings.

Left, nowadays, still means progressive, but overtime what "needs to be changed" is what defines leftism differently. There used to be a time when economy is what the leftists want to change the most, to make material distribution fairer. Now the leftism moves the battelfield to the head, the concepts. They think what keeps the society from being a good one is mostly in the thoughts. To correct it one has to remake the language, since thoughts are out of control. 

Both of these, the great theorist and beloved Chairman Mao has tried out for us. And actually, both are correct. If the sacrifice is not taken into account, it is not a bad idea to implement policies to correct both our belongings and brains. But if you take sacrifice into it, you are gonna be haunted by nightmares until the end of the universe. 

Mao is still cherished by many, and many within the country too. However, they are the offsprings of those who survived. There aren’t any speakers anymore in the graveyards under the ground, unfortunately. Otherwise we would’ve have a more balanced report of the project.

The right, however, are those who think there’s some goodness with the tradition, and something iffy with what is new. But as is also the case with the leftists, they don’t always agree on where the wrongs are. Some think that’s immigration, some technology, some the loss of religion. They also have different intensities with their beliefs, which now is called how “extreme” they are. 

The left, too, do not agree with each other on what the changes should be, but are thought of a complete whole. That introduces the interesting phenomenon that leftism seems like a giant packadge, and whoever is feminist is also gotta be a vegan, a socialist, a pro-immigration fighter, and so on. So is the case with the right. 

The minority ethinicities in a society are often placed, by those who are not themselves, as on the left. Eventually, they seem to be the target of some of the rightwings. However, many of those who go to another country appreciate its social order, which is why they go there in the first place. That certainly does not mean they think it is a perfect order or even a good one, but there is something that they think is important, which is lacking in where they come from. 

Last but not least, most people are in the middle, just like most people are what one might call “middle class”, it just comes with the normal distribution. It’s simply very impossible that people are either very this or very that. And nobody has an opnion on everything, each one takes some topics they are interested in and give opinions after research and pondering. It takes time to give an opinion and take a side, actually, a lot of time to make a decision that is not too stupid. 

In the west, it is socially obligatory to give an opinion, even on matters one does not care and has never encountered. That’s too much to ask for a person. And humanly impossibly for anyone who is not a full time thinker. Even a scholar does not have that luxury. 

What needs to change? What should remain? Does a completely new order lead us to the new age? Or is that actually an annihilation?
