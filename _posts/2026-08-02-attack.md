Can’t sleep. Writing is always cathartic for me, but I keep reminding myself to write less, otherwise there wouldn’t be as much to reserve for my papers. I write less and less and worse and worse over time. The muse has left me to another kid who is not as sly. Or I have abandoned her for a banal dream. Either way, my writing is not anymore what I would proudly share with the people I love. Nowadays, I discuss success because I have fallen this far. 

I used to get tingling on my scalp when I wrote good prose. The ones my friends would share back to me after I’ve published them. They share with me as if they did not know I wrote them, as if they would like me to enjoy myself with it as much as they did. Now I am ashamed of my words; they are dry, functional, and try to sell smartness. They repeat themselves, too. That is the most embarrassing situation. I was quite surprised when I was a bit younger to hear older people repeating their stories to me over and again. I thought they were pathetic. Now I am pathetic. Reading my own words and realising that they are just the few materials in my little brain being recounted makes me want to disappear into the vacuum. 

I just got the precursor of a gallbladder stone attack. I knew it was that because, in my short span of life, I have been attacked twice, and was in an ambulance for one of them. I knew the feeling when it is preparing, gathering its ammunition, setting up fortification, mustering a squad. It was prepared. I knew it was prepared, and I had nothing I could do. I had KFC two days in a row. I deserved it. So I got up and started writing, partially to repent my sin and hope my gallbladder forgives me. Till this moment, I think it is hesitating whether the attack should be carried out as planned. The feeling is somehow gone, but I do not know what it really thinks. 

One can hardly control her own body; that is mysterious. I am often ashamed of bodily functions, not the bizarre trend of body-shame, but function-shame. I am ashamed that I am hungry, thirsty, that I need to breathe, and that I have skin that separates me from the outside. I am so limited by the body, and am so frustrated about its unsolvability. I want to think, without sleep. But I have to sleep, and I am so unbelievably ashamed of that. And I am ashamed of my shame. That regression does not stop.

Several years ago, when I could not sleep, I would read Borges. He writes about absurdity and mathematics at the same time. He writes folklores so impossible and possible at the same time. He writes so calmly about my worst nightmares, with elegance and a touch of pepper. His writing is tangy. I cannot forget the nights when I could discard my pettiness out of a whim, when I could live for life’s sake like a swordfish. I could never forget what Borges did to me and will never do. I read the Bible a few years later, it did not give me as much blessing. 

The feeling of an attack has mostly faded. I am very proud of my gallbladder’s decision, smart and generous. But the bad news is, I cannot eat KFC again.
